02 / About

Systems first.
Code where it counts.

I'm a Saskatchewan/Alberta-based IT contractor with more than 15 years of systems administration behind me, mostly in Windows and Mac environments. The work is usually equal parts keeping the lights on and figuring out why something that should work doesn't.

The projects here are the other half of that: tools I wanted to exist. A guitar workstation that handles tab and real audio input. A betting league for friends. An encrypted messenger that forgets. I like software that runs locally, keeps its data close, and doesn't need a subscription to keep working.
